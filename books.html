Machinations of Fate is the prequel to the Titanomachia series and introduces Captain Talia Daggett of the Wings of Athena. It’s a standalone short story and not required reading for Mirror Moon, though it offers a glimpse into how Captain Talia secured the cargo-delivery and VIP escort job that we follow in Mirror Moon.

Titanomachia’s official story begins with Mirror Moon, where the main protagonists are introduced, and the central conflict is established: Can the former general in the Liberation War, Harris Ultor, save his daughter Terra as planet Olimpia descends into chaos?

Captain Talia Daggett plays a major role in Mirror Moon and the Titanomachia series, fighting to secure enough credits to pay off her loan and keep her crew alive.
